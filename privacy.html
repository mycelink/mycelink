Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what Mycelink knows about you and who can see it.

Right now

Mycelink has no member accounts yet. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details, and we do not keep a record of what you look at.

Your browser remembers the last tool you opened and your last search, so the dashboard opens where you left it. That is stored only on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The tools shown inside Mycelink

The charts come from TradingView, DexScreener, CoinMarketCap and Checkonchain. When you open one, your browser connects to that company directly, and it may set its own cookies or collect data under its own privacy policy. Mycelink does not control that. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts in the same way.

When member accounts open

Mycelink will add free member accounts. When it does:

  • You will sign in with an email address and a password. Passwords are scrambled, so nobody at Mycelink can read them.
  • Other members cannot see your profile. You see only your own.
  • The Mycelink admin can see each member's account, which tools they use, and what they save, such as watchlists and saved charts.
  • The admin uses this to understand what the community finds useful and to keep the site safe. It is not sold or shared for advertising.
  • You can ask to see your data, correct it, or have your account and its data deleted.

This page will be updated with full details, including how to contact us, before the first account can be created.
